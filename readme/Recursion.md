## 📌 Tips to Master Backtracking

- **Base Case**: Define a clear stopping condition for your recursive function.
- **Recursive Case**: Choose an option, explore it recursively, and then undo (backtrack) the choice.
- **Use Visited Sets/Arrays**: Especially when generating permutations or unique combinations.
- **Sort Input (if needed)**: Helps with pruning or skipping duplicates.
- **Draw Recursion Trees**: For difficult problems, visualize choices to understand state transitions.
